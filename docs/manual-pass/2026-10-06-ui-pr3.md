## Fixes the pass found

Each is its own commit with its own test, re-driven on Terminal.app.

1. `48394ac`: Market opened before its page loaded, so the ▶ drew on Filters. The ring's
   `autoFocus` landed there, the focus hook wrote that as the cursor, and the ▶ stayed on Filters
   once the listings came. A landing on the ▶ itself now writes nothing. Re-driven: Market opened
   on the first listing.
2. `51e0b6f`: with seven highest-zone rows and no boss damage, the boss board's heading and note
   sat on a second page that Down could never reach, because the note took no stop. The note is now
   a stop that does nothing, as the board rows are. Re-driven: seven Downs reached it, and the page
   read `9-10/10`.

## Notes

1. At 14 rows the Account detail window cuts its last line (`Linked as kevin`). At 24 rows it
   fits.
2. A key pressed less than about a second after a tab or form opens can land before the ring
   moves there. Twice it went to the old row: once an Enter right after `New buy order`, and two
   of seven Ups pressed 0.8 s apart. The machine's load average was about 20 at the time, with
   another session's headless Chromium using four cores.
3. Seven Downs on a seven-row board wrap back to the first row. That's correct; it looked like
   a stuck ring until the count was clear.
4. The probe Image sits in the zero-width stops Box. cmux took its blit there (step 1), so the
   plan's fallback (moving it into the picture's row) wasn't needed.

### Fixes the surface pass found

3. `57a2a07`: at 13 body rows the order form's price field took the picture to 12, under the
   wide floor. The pane then read "needs more room", the band took the rows it freed, and the
   next keys went to the prompt. A field that would push the picture under its floor is now left

   the ring was on.

### Notes

5. Space pressed while the ring is on one of the pane's hidden Buttons hands the keyboard to the
   prompt. The space and every key after it go there. The pre-PR 3 build (`2604bb2`) does the
   same, so it predates this PR. Inside a focused field, Space types a space.
6. System Events `keystroke` of a string with spaces drops everything from the first space on
   when typing into Ghostty, the prompt included. Single keys and `key code 49` arrive. That is a
   driving artifact, not the plugin's.
7. Standalone Ghostty at its default font draws the windows letterboxed: its cells are taller
   than the pictures' 1:2, so each Image fits by width and leaves a thin gap under it. This comes
   from the window kit (PR 1).
8. `/promptfall` opened while the composer holds text opens the pane without the keyboard, as the
   engine documents; the keys then go to the prompt.
